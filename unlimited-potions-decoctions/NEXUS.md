# Unlimited Potions & Decoctions

## Description

Cheat mod for The Witcher 3 – Next-Gen (4.0 or newer). Drink as many potions as you want without the toxicity limit, and turn on all decoctions with one toggle, no drinking needed.

## Installation instructions

**With Vortex**
1. Download the ZIP and drag it into Vortex.
2. Click **Install** and **Enable**.
3. Double-click `install.bat` in your Witcher 3 game folder once, so the menu shows up.

**Manual**
1. Unpack the ZIP.
2. Copy everything inside (`Mods`, `bin`, `install.bat`) into your Witcher 3 game folder.
3. Double-click `install.bat` there.

**Uninstall:** first set "Alle_Absude_aktiv" off and toxicity to "Normal", wait a moment and save. Then delete `Mods\modUnlimitedPotions` and `bin\config\r4game\user_config_matrix\pc\modUnlimitedPotions.xml`.

## Main features

Settings in game: **Options → Mods → Cheats → Unlimited_Potions**

- **Toxicity from potions** (Gift_durch_Traenke):
  - **Kein_Gift** (default): potions give no toxicity at all.
  - **1_pro_Trank**: each potion gives only 1 toxicity (good for the "Euphoria" mutation).
  - **Normal**: like the normal game.
- **All decoctions active** (Alle_Absude_aktiv): all 28 decoctions work permanently, no drinking needed. On by default.
- Decoctions you drink yourself no longer lock toxicity.
- Works in old saves, takes effect after about 2 seconds.
- No Script Merger needed (uses script annotations).

## Requirements

- The Witcher 3 – Next-Gen, version 4.0 or newer.
- No other mods needed.
- Do not combine with other mods that change toxicity or decoctions.
